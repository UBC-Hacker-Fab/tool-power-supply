Known analyzer artifacts on this design (not design errors):

- RS-001 "no declared source" on 12V_MOD: the analyzer found no power-output pin or PWR_FLAG driving it, so it cannot trace the rail back to the U1 module.
- 6 VM-001 "voltage domain crossing" errors on `EN.5V_A`, `EN.5V_B`, `FAULT.5V`, `I2C.ALERT`, `__unnamed_54`, `__unnamed_55`. These look like artifacts: the analyzer takes a net's voltage from its name (so `EN.5V_A` and `SENSE.12V_A` read as 5 V and 12 V although they are 3.3 V logic), and from the rail an INA226 is attached to (its Vbus and Vin pins sit on the monitored rail while its logic runs from 3V3(OUT)). Renaming the bus members (for example `EN.TAP5A`) would remove the first kind.
